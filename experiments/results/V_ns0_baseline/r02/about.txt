Video trial for the disturbance case, null-space mode 0.

Plain pose hold at the table posture with the automatic point force on link 3,
at the largest push the controller's own validation allows: 40 N is the ceiling
in validateAutomaticDisturbance, applied 0.15 m along the link z axis so the
resulting joint torque reaches the 5 N m cap that is the other ceiling.

Raised over 3 to 5 s, held to 13 s, released over a 1 s ramp, and the run
continues to 24 s. The window is longer than the 20 N runs in r01: at 20 N over
3 s the redundant axis moved 0.6 deg, which is not visible on camera, so the
force now acts for 10 s and the elbow travel accumulates.

What it shows: the redundant axis with no null-space torque at all. This is the
reference the two other video runs are watched against.
