# T-mode quasi-static repeat with explicit K-frame wrench logging

Select t at the startup menu. Do not press g or p during the run.
Keep the tool clear of the surface for the first baseline, then perform the
following motions while watching the controller time:

- 0.5--3.0 s: leave the tool untouched and clear of the surface;
- 3.5--7.5 s: push slowly by approximately 20 mm along the surface normal;
- 8.0--12.0 s: hold the normal displacement stationary;
- 12.0--17.0 s: maintain the same normal displacement without rotating;
- 17.0--21.0 s: maintain the normal displacement and rotate approximately
  5 deg about positive tangent 1;
- 22.0--28.0 s: hold both displacement and rotation stationary;
- 28.0--34.0 s: release the tool and leave it untouched.

The run preserves the original stiffnesses, enables unit-ratio automatic
damping, disables the compliance-centre shift, and disables null-space torque.
The log contains the calculated damping, both O_F_ext_hat_K and
K_F_ext_hat_K, and the measured K-to-TCP offset.
