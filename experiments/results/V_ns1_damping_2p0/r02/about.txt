Video trial for the disturbance case, null-space mode 1.

Identical to V_ns0_baseline with projected null-space damping at
nullspace_damping = 2.0 N m s/rad.

What it shows: the elbow swing is damped while the disturbance acts, but
damping alone has no preferred posture, so the arm keeps whatever elbow angle
the push left it in. At the 20 N push of r01 the damping law produced 0.058 N m
and this run was indistinguishable from mode 0; the larger push is what gives
it a velocity to work against.
