Video trial for the disturbance case, null-space mode 1.

Identical to V_ns0_baseline with projected null-space damping at
nullspace_damping = 20.0 N m s/rad.

The earlier setup at 2.0 N m s/rad is kept under V_ns1_damping_2p0, where its
two archived trials record what that value did: nothing visible. Damping
opposes null-space velocity, so under a steady push the elbow settles into a
slow drift the damping torque barely resists, and mode 1 swung 22.6 deg against
mode 0's 22.7. Raising the coefficient tenfold cuts the drift velocity by the
same factor, which is what makes the mode readable on camera.

Note that 20.0 is far outside the 1.0 to 4.0 the coefficient was ever swept at.
Nothing in the controller clamps it, and the law multiplies measured joint
velocity, so watch the commanded torque for chatter.
