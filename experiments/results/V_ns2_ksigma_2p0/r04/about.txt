Video trial for the disturbance case, null-space mode 2.

Identical to V_ns0_baseline with the smallest-singular-value bias only, at
nullspace_sigma_gain = 2.0 N m. Undamped, so the elbow is driven rather than
held.

What it shows: whether the commanded null-space direction is actually followed
by joint motion. Torque without motion is joint friction, not conditioning. In
r01 this run drove the elbow the opposite way from the push while modes 0 and 1
did not move at all.
