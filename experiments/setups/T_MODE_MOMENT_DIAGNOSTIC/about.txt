# Contact-Impedance Hold moment diagnostic

This is a separate diagnostic pilot.  It is not a repetition of the reported
contact campaign.  The centre of compliance is at the TCP and null-space
torque is disabled.  The impedance is unchanged from the first consistency
trial: (K_{p,n}=1000\,\mathrm{N/m}),
(D_{p,n}=175\,\mathrm{N\,s/m}),
(K_{R,t_1}=15\,\mathrm{N\,m/rad}), and
(D_{R,t_1}=10.01\,\mathrm{N\,m\,s/rad}).

Operator timing from the hold clock:

- 0--4 s: leave the robot untouched;
- 4--9 s: push slowly along the configured surface normal to about 20 mm;
- 10--15 s: keep the normal displacement stationary;
- 15--20 s: keep the same normal load and do not rotate;
- 20--26 s: rotate slowly to about 5 deg about tangent 1 while retaining the
  normal load;
- 27--34 s: keep both the displacement and rotation stationary;
- 34--36 s: release smoothly.

Do not chase the nominal values.  Stop below them if the motion feels unsafe,
the tool slips, or the robot reports a contact/reflex condition.  The analysis
uses the achieved displacement and rotation.

The comparison uses matched references.  Normal-force increments are measured
from the untouched 0.5--3.0 s baseline.  Moment increments are measured from
the loaded 17.0--20.0 s baseline, on both the command and estimator sides.  It
also retains the absolute TCP-referenced estimator moment.  The physical
contact point and any contact couple remain unobserved, so the test cannot
separate a force lever from a local contact moment.
