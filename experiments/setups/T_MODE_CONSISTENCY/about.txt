# T-mode quasi-static wrench-consistency check

The run starts directly in setup-impedance hold (`t`).  It records a no-touch
baseline, a held displacement of approximately 10 mm along the configured
surface normal, and a held rotation of approximately 10 deg about tangent 1.
The compliance-centre shift and null-space torque are disabled for this check.

Timing from the controller clock:

- 0.0--3.0 s: no touch;
- 3.0--6.0 s: move slowly to 10 mm along the surface normal;
- 6.0--10.0 s: hold the normal displacement still;
- 10.0--13.0 s: release and leave the robot untouched;
- 13.0--16.0 s: rotate slowly to 10 deg about tangent 1;
- 16.0--20.0 s: hold the rotation still;
- 20.0--26.0 s: release and leave the robot untouched.

The sign of either displacement may be positive or negative.  The analysis
retains that sign and compares like-signed commanded and model-estimated
components in the configured surface frame.
