# T-mode repeat with the original manual damping

Select t at the startup menu. Do not press g or p during the run.

- 0.5--3.0 s: leave the tool untouched;
- 3.5--7.5 s: push approximately 20 mm along the surface normal;
- 8.0--13.0 s: hold the normal displacement stationary;
- 13.0--17.0 s: maintain the same push without rotating;
- 17.0--22.0 s: rotate approximately 5 deg about positive tangent 1;
- 22.0--31.0 s: maintain the displacement and angle, applying only the
  counter-torque needed to prevent return;
- 31.0--34.0 s: release the tool.

This setup restores the original manual impedance gains, including
DR_t1=10.01 N m s/rad. Automatic damping, the compliance-centre shift and
null-space torque are disabled. The controller logs both O_F_ext_hat_K and
K_F_ext_hat_K, together with the K-to-TCP offset.
